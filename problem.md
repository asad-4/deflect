# Problem

People who enjoy challenging, skill-based combat games like Sekiro often want something they can play directly in their browser without needing a gaming PC, console, or downloading a large game. Most browser fighting games feel too simple and rely on button-mashing rather than actual skill. They don't really capture the satisfaction of learning an enemy's attack patterns, timing your deflections perfectly, and finally winning a difficult fight. Players who enjoy that kind of combat don't have many quick, accessible browser games that offer the same experience.

# Initial requirements

- R1: Let the player move, attack, block, and dodge using keyboard controls.
- R2: Include a perfect deflect system that rewards blocking at the right moment and penalizes poor timing.
- R3: Give both the player and enemy posture bars that increase when attacks are blocked or deflected. A full posture bar leaves the fighter vulnerable.
- R4: Include an AI-controlled boss with several recognizable attack patterns and visual warnings so players can learn when to block, deflect, or dodge.
- R5: Include at least one unblockable attack that the player must dodge instead of blocking.
- R6: Allow the player to perform a finishing blow when the boss's posture breaks, which wins the fight.
- R7: Give both the player and the boss a health bar. Taking a hit lowers health, and the player loses when their health reaches zero.
- R8: Display a win or lose screen at the end of the fight with an option to restart.
